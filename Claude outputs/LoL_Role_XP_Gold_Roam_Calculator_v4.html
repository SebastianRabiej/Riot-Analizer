    Where the gold comes from

    Lane minion gold assumes you last-hit. Melee 20g, caster 14g, cannon 50g +1g per 90s. Plates are permanent since 26.1 and lose 10g per minute from 11:00 (max −40g). Support gold comes from World Atlas charges, not last hits.

    300–420gNormal champion kill · scales with victim level
    102gStandard 3 melee + 3 caster wave
    120 → 80gOuter plate, decays 11:00–15:00 · inner & inhibitor: 600g over 5 plates, no decay · split with nearby allies
    18g / chargeWorld Atlas: 1 charge per 20s, max 3 · ADC keeps the minion gold

    Assumptions and limits

    Champion kills: 300g at victim levels 1–6, +10g per level after that. First Blood, bounties and shutdowns are excluded. Assist gold is a pool of 50% of the base bounty, split between credited assisters. Kill XP uses the 2026 level-gap rule (±20% per level beyond the first, minimum 40%), and shared kill XP uses 66.6% / 82% / 90% of solo XP by level band.

    Lane minions (26.1): Melee 62 XP / 20g, caster 31 XP / 14g, cannon 75 XP / 50g +1g per 90s. Minion XP share per champion is 100 / 65 / 43.3 / 32.5% for 1–4 champions. Waves come every 30s, 25s from 14:00 and 20s from 30:00. Cannons come every 3rd wave, every 2nd from 14:00 and every wave from 25:00. From 14:00 cannon waves have one fewer melee, and from 30:00 one fewer caster as well. Jungle-item users get 30% → 100% minion XP between 0:00 and 20:00. The 25% off-lane penalty before level 3 is not modelled.

    Roam model: Lost waves = (time away − free window) ÷ wave interval. The free window is 1.2 intervals if you crashed, 0.4 if neutral, and 0 plus half a wave lost to your tower if the wave is pushing to you. Death cost = base respawn by level (≈+2% per minute after 15:00, capped at +50%) plus about 25s walking, valued at your farm rate. In team mode, the killer's gold, allies' XP shares, your ADC's extra XP (support roams), the enemy's bounty on you and lost plates are added. Jungle camps wait for you, so only 50% of gank time counts as lost farm. Combined value = gold + XP × your XP value. Estimated levels assume about 85% XP uptake from waves or camps.

    Turrets (26.1): Every turret has 5 plates that break at 10 / 25 / 45 / 70 / 100% missing HP. So plates need 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30% of turret HP for the same gold, and plate 5 is the turret kill. Outer plates are 120g (−10g/min from 11:00, min 80g). Riot lists inner and inhibitor turrets as 600g over 5 plates; an even 120g split is assumed. Turret local gold is now 0, and the first turret adds 300g. Global gold on destruction is not modelled. Nexus turrets have plates too, but no gold value is published.

    Uncertain values: Patch 26.1 notes say plates are permanent; older wiki pages still say they fall at 14:00. Respawn timers and the cannon cadence after 14:00 come from community sources. Support quest gold differs between sources, so it is not added.
